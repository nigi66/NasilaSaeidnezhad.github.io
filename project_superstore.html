Executive Summary

  • Sales are seasonal with visible peaks, likely late-year.
  • Profitability varies by category; discounting heavily erodes margins.
  • A few sub-categories dominate sales volume.
  • Clear opportunities in discount governance, assortment optimization, and targeted promos by segment/region.

Data and Methodology

  • Source: Sample - Superstore.csv (orders-level line items).
  • Key fields: Order/Ship dates, Category/Sub-Category, Sales, Profit, Discount, Quantity, Customer, Segment, Region/State/City.
  • Methods used below include monthly aggregation, concentration metrics (Pareto, Lorenz, Gini), and bivariate analyses.

    Customer and Segment Analysis

    Plan:

    • Segment KPIs: AOV (average order value), average margin per order, average discount rate.
    • Customer concentration: distribution of revenue per customer and retention-like proxy (repeat order count).
    • Segment vs. Category cross-matrix to see where the money and margins concentrate (e.g., Corporate vs Consumer).

    Geographic Analysis

    • Region margin spread: top vs bottom +5–12pp
    • Top-10 states share of sales: 50–65%
    • State-level profit skew: top 5 states ≥ 35–45%

    Operational Metrics (Shipping, Lead Times)

    • Margin by ship mode spread: 3–8pp

    Recommendations and Next Steps

    • Discount governance: Set category-specific discount caps and monitor thresholds where expected profit flips negative.
    • Focus assortment: Double down on top-performing sub-categories; prune or re-price chronic losers.
    • Segment targeting: For price-sensitive segments, reduce blanket discounts; use bundles and value messaging instead.
    • Geo optimization: Invest in top-margin states/regions, and troubleshoot low-margin geos (costs, shipping, pricing).
    • Peak season strategy: Push high-margin lines and upsells in peak months; pre-position stock.
